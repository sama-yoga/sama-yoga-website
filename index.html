About

Sama

Sama. This word sits at the beginning of my name. In Tagalog, a language of my own cultural roots, sama means to go with. To accompany. Kasama is the one who walks beside you. Samahan is both the accompanying and the community that grows from it. In my yoga studies, I have come across sama in Sanskrit as well, where it points toward evenness, levelness, balance. While I do not come from a South Asian lineage, I hold those teachings with gratitude and reverence. I find it such a beautiful thing: that two languages quite distant from one another arrive at something so near to what I hope a practice can offer... steadiness and togetherness. That is what I am trying to make room for.

What I hope to offer as a teacher is simple, and it is the thing my name has been telling me all along: a little steadiness, a little grounding, and the sense that you are not moving through it alone. Steadiness and company. Sama, in both of its meanings. I would be honored to practice alongside you.

Meet Samantha Marquez (she/they)

Samantha Marquez began practicing in 2011, though yoga became something else entirely during her journey through infertility. In a season when her body felt like a stranger, and at times an adversary, the practice offered her a way back in, not toward fixing anything, only toward being with what was already there. That way back kept opening through pregnancy, birth, and postpartum with her two children, and it is still opening today.

She trained with the faculty at the SOMA School of Yoga and Somatics at The Bhakti Yoga Movement Center, where the grounding in trauma healing, experiential anatomy, accessibility, and decolonizing yoga shaped both how and why she teaches. She is currently pursuing an 85-hour Prenatal & Postpartum Yoga and Movement Certification with Next Wave Birth. Outside the studio, she has spent over a decade as an educational and programming facilitator, drawn to how learning takes root and how a room of individuals becomes a collective, the same thing she loves watching happen on the mat.
